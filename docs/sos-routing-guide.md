# SOS routing guide

SOS is the three kinds of call the desk can book for a customer instead of answering in writing:

- Onboarding call: a thirty-minute setup call with someone on the desk. Offer it when a self-service account under sixty days old writes in about setup: staff accounts, invitations, importing a staff list, the first rota, time-off rules.
- Rota-planning consultation: an hour with a lead to plan rotas across venues, roles and opening hours. Offer it when a self-service account asks about more than one venue, about roles, about staff who work at several sites, or about anything that lives on the team or venue plans.
- Prospect demo: a demo with sales. Offer it when the writer is not yet a customer (no account, or a trial account) and asks what the product does, what it costs, or whether it covers their case.

Self-service tickets are tickets from accounts on the starter plan, whatever channel they came in on. Accounts on the team and venue plans have an account manager and are not routed through SOS.

A missed opportunity is a self-service ticket where one of the three rules above applied and the reply neither offered the call nor booked it. A reply that offers the call and is turned down is not a miss.
