# QA criteria

Ten yes/no questions for a solved ticket. Score each one 1 (yes) or 0 (no); the ticket's score is the sum out of 10.

1. Personal opening: the reply opens with the customer's name and a greeting written for them, not a template.
2. Relevant questions: where the first message left something unclear, the reply asks the one question that resolves it instead of guessing.
3. Empathy: the reply acknowledges the problem in the customer's own terms before the fix.
4. Relationship: the reply refers to the account (the venue, their plan, what they asked before) where it helps.
5. Process: the fix follows our help-centre article for that problem, or the reply says why it does not.
6. Spelling: no spelling or grammar mistakes.
7. Ownership: the reply says who is doing what and by when; nothing is left as "someone will look into it".
8. Correct drop-downs and notes: the ticket's category matches the problem and the handler's name is on it.
9. Resolution: the problem is fixed or the customer is told plainly that it is not, with the next step.
10. Proactive education: the reply points at the article or the setting that stops the problem coming back.
